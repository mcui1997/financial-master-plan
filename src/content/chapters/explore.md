## For the tinkerers

Some people enjoy markets. They want to pick stocks, hold more Bitcoin, try a strategy, or follow a hunch. Telling them "never do that" doesn't work. They do it anyway, often with too much money.

So this plan gives it a fenced-off space: the **explore slice**.

## The rules

1. **10% of your investments, at most.** The other 90% or more follows the core plan.
2. **Only money you could lose completely** without changing your life.
3. **It never borrows from the core.** If the slice drops, you don't top it up from your plan.
4. **Trim it back when it grows.** If a lucky run takes the slice above 10%, move the extra into the core plan. That locks in the win.
5. **Long-term money only.** Never your emergency fund. Never money you need within 5 years.

## Why the fence matters

The core plan works because it is boring and steady. The explore slice lets you scratch the itch without putting that at risk. If it goes to zero, your retirement is barely touched. If it soars, it helps, and the trimming rule turns that into real, lasting wealth.

## An honest note

Most people who trade actively do worse than a plain index fund, after fees and taxes. Many professional fund managers do too. If you don't feel the urge, **don't open an explore slice.** Your core plan is complete without it.

If you do, keep score honestly. Once a year, compare your slice to what it would have made in your core mix. If it keeps losing, that is useful information too.

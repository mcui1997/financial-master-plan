## You are not behind

Most people were never taught how money works. Not in school, and often not at home. If this feels new, that is normal. It does not mean you are late. It means you are starting.

You do not need to be good at math. You do not need to watch the news. You need one simple plan and the patience to stick with it.

## The whole plan in five lines

1. **Know your numbers** and build a cash cushion for emergencies.
2. **Fill the right accounts in the right order.** Free money first.
3. **Pick a mix** of a few simple funds based on where you are in life.
4. **Buy a little every payday,** automatically, no matter what the market is doing.
5. **Tidy up once a year,** and shift your mix toward safety as retirement gets closer.

That is it. The rest of this site explains each step, why it works, and how to do it.

## What this site is

- **A complete plan for regular people.** It covers saving, investing, taxes, crashes, and retirement.
- **Written in plain words.** When a money word shows up, it is underlined. Tap it for a quick definition.
- **Hands-on.** Most chapters have a small tool. Put in your own numbers and see what happens.
- **Private.** Anything you type stays in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. No tracking, no ads.

## What this site is not

- **Not personal advice.** It does not know your full situation. Use it to learn and to plan, then make your own decisions.
- **Not a way to get rich quick.** The plan is slow and boring on purpose. Boring is what works.
- **Not the only right answer.** Smart people invest in different ways. Where this plan differs from common advice, we say so and explain why.

## How to use it

Go in order if you are new. Each chapter builds on the last one. Each one starts with **the short version**, so you always know where it is heading.

Short on time? Go back to the front page and pick where you are in life. You will get a shorter reading path made for you.

At the end of most chapters, there are one or two **check yourself** questions. They are not a test. They just show you whether the idea landed.

When you are ready, open **My Plan**. It pulls your answers from every tool into one page you can print or save.

## Why it feels hard, and why it isn't

Money talk is full of noise. Hot stocks, crypto news, people on video saying the crash is coming. It makes investing feel like a game you have to win every day.

It is not. The people who do well are rarely the smartest traders. They are the ones who picked a sensible plan, set it up once, and left it alone for decades.

That is what you will build here.
